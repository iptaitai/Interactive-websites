Arrange the Investigation Steps

Drag the cards into the correct sequence for testing Objects A, B and C. On touch devices or with a keyboard, use the ↑ and ↓ buttons.

  1. ☰🧠Decide whether Object A is a magnet, magnetic material or non-magnetic material.
  2. ☰👀Observe whether Object A is attracted, repelled or not attracted.
  3. ☰🔁Repeat for Objects B and C.
  4. ☰🔵Bring the South pole close to the same end of Object A.
  5. ☰📝Record the observation.
  6. ☰🧲Bring the North pole of the bar magnet close to one end of Object A.
  7. ☰👀Observe whether Object A is attracted, repelled or not attracted.
